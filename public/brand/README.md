# Relay logo

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. `relay-source.png` preserves the final transparent source; `relay-logo.png` is the 256px app asset. Browser and Apple icons are exported to `app/icon.png` and `app/apple-icon.png` on white for legibility in browser chrome.

Original prompt:

Create one finished standalone logo symbol for Relay, a professional AI chat workspace that routes tasks to the right agent and model. A restrained charcoal and warm-white interface. A distinctive compact abstract relay/routing monogram suggesting an R through two interlocking broad rounded paths and a precise negative-space channel, conveying a handoff and forward motion. Flat monochrome ink, bold simple silhouette, geometrically disciplined, soft corners, recognizable at 20px. Centered square composition, true transparent background. No wordmark, gradients, shadow, 3D, hairlines, decorative frame, or mockup.

Final refinement prompt:

Preserve the R routing monogram silhouette and proportions. Make it clean flat solid charcoal (#181818) ink for white and light gray surfaces. Remove all speckles, scraps, fringe, mottling, and stray pixels. Clean negative space, uniformly filled silhouette, smooth antialiased edges. No textures, shadows, or gradients. Center with balanced clear padding on a transparent background. One production logo symbol, no text or mockup.
